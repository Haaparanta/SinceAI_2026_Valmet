## Section by section

### 1. What it is

An immersive **360° digital twin** that replicates the customer's exact machinery and facility layout. Three uses:

- prepare operators and maintenance staff for the **initial start-up**
- prepare them for **major shutdowns**
- keep skills sharp with **continuous maintenance-scenario training** across all process areas

### 2. Learning through experience

Three claimed benefits:

- higher knowledge retention
- lifelike maintenance scenarios
- improved workplace safety

### 3. Enter a 360 learning environment

Users wear virtual reality equipment with hand controllers, move freely through the 3D setting and interact with simulated machinery to learn the process and equipment. The shipped training modules are lifelike scenarios for **maintenance work in the forming and press sections** of a paper or board machine. Learning by personal experience is said to improve retention, job performance and safety.

A quote from **Katriina Geijer, Senior Manager, Customer Training at Valmet**: Valmet wants customers to get the most from their equipment and looks for more efficient knowledge transfer, because a mill's success depends on its people's competence as much as on technology.

## Glossary

- **Digital design twin**: a 3D model built from the machine's design data (CAD), as opposed to a scan.
- **Valmet Performance Center**: Valmet's remote expert support centre.
- **Centerline Advisor**: compares live process values with the best past operating point.
- **Forming / press section**: the first two sections of a paper machine, where the sheet is formed and water is pressed out.

## Relevance to our build

- **Valmet already sells "walk through a digital plant to a maintenance task".** Our demo does the same thing from a quick 3DGS scan instead of an expensive CAD-built twin. That is a strong pitch line: *scan in an hour, get a navigable, semantic twin*.
- **Centerline = baseline.** Comparing each asset's latest telemetry with its own healthy baseline (first day of the sample, or the healthy assets of the same class) is the same idea as Centerline Advisor, and it is easy to explain.
- **Training mode** is a cheap extra: replay the walk to the faulty pump as a guided onboarding tour.
- **Caveat:** this is a paper-machine product built from design data. Borrow the concept, not the data model.
